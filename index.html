📡 VerifiedPulse

Logical Fact-Checking System

VerifiedPulse uses mathematical propositional logic and live web evidence to evaluate claims. Type or paste any claim, our engine searches for corroborating sources, evaluates two fixed propositions P (official statement) and Q (credible source), applies conjunction logic (p ∧ q), and determines verification status.

🔍 Fact Checker Text Claim (Conjunction Logic)

P: The claim is backed by an official statement, announcement, or memorandum.
Q: The information is reported or confirmed by a credible news outlet, fact-checking organization, or recognized expert institution.
Active Provider: Ready

𝒾 Truth Table

All combinations of propositions P and Q and their AND result. The claim is Verified only when every proposition is True.

How it works: The system uses Conjunction Logic (p ∧ q) with two fixed propositions: P (official statement) and Q (credible source). For a Verified (True) status, both propositions must be true. If either is False, the conjunction evaluates to False and the claim is flagged as Unverified / High Misinformation Risk.
